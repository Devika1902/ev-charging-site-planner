01
Transparent weighted scoreAll component scores use a 0–100 scale.
Score = Demand × w₁ + Traffic × w₂ + EV adoption × w₃ + Supply gap × w₄

Each signal is normalized to a comparable scale; the weights shown in Overview are normalized to sum to 100%. Demand potential combines EV adoption and population density. The gap signal rewards unmet demand relative to nearby fast-charging supply. This is a screening tool, not a financial forecast.

⚠ A high traffic score may reflect through-traffic with little stopping behaviour. Validate dwell time and access before interpreting it as charging demand.

Sensitivity & validationChallenge the ranking before acting on it.

Change the scoring priorities in Overview and observe rank changes. A candidate that falls sharply under reasonable weight changes should be treated as assumption-sensitive. Validate charger inventory, connector status, traffic timing, land access, grid capacity and permitting with local evidence.

03
Data quality & limitationsKnow what could invalidate this shortlist.

The included demo records are synthetic and have no connection to official statistics. Open Charge Map is useful for exploratory competitor mapping, but it is not a complete authoritative inventory; record-level licensing and attribution vary. Regional EV registrations do not directly measure charging sessions. Population and traffic are demand proxies, and hypothetical city-area candidates do not imply land rights or Allego plans.
